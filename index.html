Guidelines & Algorithms
Interactive References
📐
Chamber Quantification
2015 ASE/EACVI guidelines — normal values for LV size, EF, mass, GLS, RV dimensions and function, LA/RA volumes, aortic root, and IVC. Includes interactive calculators for EF, LV mass, RWT, FAC, and RIMP.
Lang et al., JASE 2015 Open →
💜
Right Heart & Pulmonary Hypertension
Interactive PH probability grading, normal value tables, RV function grading, RAP algorithm, pre- vs. post-capillary phenotyping, and WSPH group classification.
Mukherjee et al., JASE 2025 Open →
🟢
Diastolic Function
Step-by-step 2025 ASE diastolic function algorithm with LAP estimation, DD grading, special populations (AF, HCM, MR, MAC, PH), and normal ranges by age.
Nagueh et al., JASE 2025 Open →
📊
Strain Echocardiography
2025 ASE/EACVI consensus on myocardial deformation imaging — GLS acquisition, vendor variability, reference values, and clinical applications across cardiomyopathies, chemotherapy, and valvular disease.
Thomas et al., JASE 2025 Open →
🔩
Prosthetic Valve Function
Stenosis and regurgitation criteria for aortic, mitral, tricuspid, and pulmonary prostheses. Includes PPM thresholds, SVD criteria, hemodynamic calculators, multimodality imaging guidance, and normal reference values (Tables A1–A9) by valve type and size.
Zoghbi et al., JASE 2024 Open →
🏃
Stress Echocardiography
ASE guidelines for stress echo in ischemic heart disease — protocols (treadmill, bicycle, DSE), interpretation, diastolic stress testing, hemodynamic assessment in AS and MR, myocardial viability, and risk stratification.
Pellikka et al., JASE 2020 · Nagueh et al., JASE 2025 Open →
🫀
Hypertrophic Cardiomyopathy
2022 ASE multimodality imaging guidelines — LVH phenotypes, LVOT gradient calculator, SCD risk stratification, family screening algorithm, diastolic function, and septal reduction therapy guidance.
Nagueh et al., JASE 2022 Open →

Quantitative Tools
🔵
MR & TR Quantification
PISA/EROA, continuity equation, regurgitant volume and fraction for mitral and tricuspid regurgitation. Severity grading per AHA/ACC and ESC guidelines.
AHA/ACC 2021 · ESC/EACTS 2021 Open →
❤️
Aortic Stenosis
AVA by continuity equation, dimensionless index, projected AVA, low-flow/low-gradient classification, and dobutamine stress echo adjudication of pseudo-severe AS.
AHA/ACC 2021 · ESC/EACTS 2021 Open →
🟡
Echo Swan
Noninvasive intracardiac pressure estimation. Calculates RAP, RVSP, PASP, PAEDP, mPAP (4 methods), PVR, LAP, LVSP, and LVEDP with a hemodynamic summary.
Echocardiographic Pressure Estimation Open →
